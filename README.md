# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application that calculates
the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount,
rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal Amount
- R = Rate of Interest
- T = Time Period

Total Amount:

Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## Example

If:

- Principal = 10000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = 1000

Total Amount = 10000 + 1000

Total Amount = 11000

## Features

- Calculates simple interest
- Calculates total amount
- Easy to understand
- Simple and user-friendly
